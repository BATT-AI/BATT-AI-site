01 Nov 2025

BATT-AI project officially launched

The consortium has launched its NWO-funded project on AI-enabled large-scale battery operation.

Project launch

The BATT-AI project officially started on 1 November 2025 as part of the NWO Open Technology Programme 2024. The project is funded by the Netherlands Organisation for Scientific Research (NWO) and focuses on the next generation of intelligent battery energy storage systems for the Dutch energy transition.

Participants and roles

The consortium brings together TU Delft, University of Twente, Stedin, Alfen, Eneco, ENTRNCE and KYOS. The partners combine expertise in power electronics, distribution grids, energy markets, battery operation, analytics and hardware-oriented validation.

Expected goals and vision

BATT-AI aims to develop AI-enabled battery intelligence that can alleviate grid congestion, provide inertia support and improve profitability in electricity market participation. The project will develop dynamic capacity models, market mechanisms, model predictive control tools and grid-forming primary control strategies, then validate the integrated approach through simulation, laboratory testing and power hardware-in-the-loop demonstrations.

The long-term vision is to make large-scale batteries active grid assets: not only storing energy, but also unlocking flexible connection capacity, supporting stable grid operation and creating reliable value streams for battery owners, system operators and technology providers.
