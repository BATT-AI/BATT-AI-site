Innovations

Integrated methods for grid capacity modelling, multi-market battery decision support and inertia provision.

BATT-AI focuses on three innovation areas: grid capacity modelling, decision support for multi-market battery participation and inertia provision with BESS.

IN1

Connection capacity optimisation

Connection limits and market mechanisms

Models that dynamically quantify and optimise connection capacity downstream of substations by combining real network data with uncertainty-aware optimisation.

  • Dynamic connection-capacity limits at fine temporal resolution.
  • Identification and utilisation of unused contracted capacity.
  • Design and assessment of connection-capacity market mechanisms.
IN2

Decision support under uncertainty

Risk-aware MPC for multi-market participation

Advances MPC for BESS operation by accounting for epistemic uncertainty, degradation and state-of-health (SoH) impacts, supporting participation across multiple markets while maintaining technical feasibility.

  • Explicit treatment of epistemic uncertainty and degradation.
  • SoH-aware operation for long-term battery performance.
  • Risk-aware participation across energy and ancillary markets.
IN3

Optimising inertia market participation

Contract strategies for inertia market participation

Models that assess the operational characteristics and market value of BESS inertia provision by considering battery constraints, degradation and long-term contracting decisions.

  • Assessment of BESS operational characteristics and degradation under inertia provision.
  • Evaluation of the value of stacking inertia with other BESS services.
  • Optimisation of capacity allocation, contracting and service-stacking strategies.
